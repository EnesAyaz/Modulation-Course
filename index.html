EJ2311 Modulation of Power Electronic Converters

KTH · 6 credits

Modulation of power converters plays a decisive role in modern power electronics, where low losses, dynamic requirements, harmonics and EMI all matter. The course gives a working understanding of modern synthesis and analysis methods of modulation for voltage source converters: carrier-based PWM, zero-vector placement and space vector modulation, overmodulation, selective harmonic elimination, direct torque control and multilevel modulation.
