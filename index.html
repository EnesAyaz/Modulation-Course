Modulation of power electronic converters

Interactive visualisations for EJ2311 at KTH. Each example opens the PWM Explorer with the settings from a lecture, computer assignment or lab task. You see the converter circuit with its switch states, the switched waveform, the harmonic spectrum and the space vectors, and you can change any parameter from there.

Converters: three-phase two-level, single-phase H-bridge, three-level NPC and cascaded H-bridge. Methods: sine PWM, third-harmonic injection, SVM, DPWM1, six-step and selective harmonic elimination.
