Learning outcomes

After the course, the student should be able to:

  • Apply Fourier series expansions to analyse arbitrary periodic signals in the frequency domain.
  • Compute all electrical quantities in symmetric LTI three-phase networks energised by periodic three-phase sources.
  • Use waveform symmetries to identify spectral components in periodic signals.
  • Compute common figures of merit for harmonic distortion, notably THD and WTHD.
  • Design carrier-based PWM modulators for single- and polyphase voltage source converters, with natural or regular sampling.
  • Explain how phase shifting the carrier and reference affects the harmonics in phase and line-to-line quantities.
  • Describe different kinds of zero-vector placement in three-phase carrier-based PWM.
  • Explain the operation of a simple space vector modulator and its analogies with carrier-based modulators.
  • Choose sets of harmonics to eliminate in selective harmonic elimination and compute the switching angles.
  • Explain the qualitative differences between carrier-based and programmed PWM in harmonic and dynamic properties.
  • Design and simulate simple direct torque control systems, including the switch table.
  • Draw and explain the most common multilevel converter topologies and the synthesis of carrier-based multilevel waveforms.

Teaching activities

  • Lectures, 16 hours in total, given in English. Attendance is voluntary.
  • Tutorials, six 2-hour sessions where problems are solved in interaction with the students, as preparation for the exam.
  • Computer exercises, two, on numerical synthesis and analysis of modulation patterns. A preparatory assignment is handed out at the first lecture and handed in at the start of the first exercise. Each report is uploaded to Canvas and discussed in an oral session of about 30 minutes.
  • Laboratory exercise (compulsory), on a three-phase SiC converter feeding an induction machine.

Examination

To pass the course you must pass both computer exercise reports, the laboratory report and the written exam. Registration for the exam is compulsory.
