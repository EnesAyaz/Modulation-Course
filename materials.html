MATLAB code

The course uses the power converter modulation toolbox (modlab): carrier generators (carrtri, carrsawp, carrsawn), modulators (mod_nspwm, mod_svm, …), and analytical spectra by double Fourier series (anaspect2lnsc, spect2lanalyt). It is distributed via Canvas.
