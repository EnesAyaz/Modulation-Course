Conventions

Units follow Holmes & Lipo: the dc link is 2Vdc with midpoint z, so every phase output lies between −Vdc and +Vdc, and M = 1 gives a fundamental of Vdc in vaz. In the cascaded H-bridge each cell has Vdc/N, and z is the star point of the strings. All voltages are shown per unit of Vdc. Harmonic amplitudes are peak values. WTHD0 = √(Σn≥2(Vn/n)²) divided by the fundamental at M = 1 (Vdc for phase quantities, √3·Vdc for three-phase line-to-line, 2Vdc for the H-bridge), and WTHD = WTHD0 / M. The R-L load has its impedance normalised to 1 at the fundamental; the three-phase load is star-connected with an isolated neutral. The flux is ψ = ∫vαβ dθ, in per unit of Vdc/ω1. Multilevel carriers are triangular with natural sampling.
