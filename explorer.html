Conventions

Units follow Holmes & Lipo: the dc link is 2Vdc with midpoint z, so each phase leg switches between ±Vdc, and M = 1 gives a fundamental of Vdc in vaz. All voltages are shown per unit of Vdc. Harmonic amplitudes are peak values. WTHD0 = √(Σn≥2(Vn/n)²) divided by the fundamental at M = 1 (Vdc for phase quantities, √3·Vdc for line-to-line), and WTHD = WTHD0 / M. The R-L load is star-connected with an isolated neutral; its impedance is normalised to 1 at the fundamental. The flux is ψ = ∫vαβ dθ, in per unit of Vdc/ω1.
